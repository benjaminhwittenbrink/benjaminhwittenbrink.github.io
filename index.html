Hello!


I am a PhD student in Economics at MIT. My research interests are in applied microeconomics, with a focus in industrial organization, political economy, and public economics.


Previously, I worked as a quantiative researcher at QuantCo in San Francisco. Before that, I graduated from Stanford with a BA in Economics with Honors, a BS in Mathematics, and a MS in Computer Science. During my masters, I worked as a research assistant with Matthew Gentzkow on projects surrounding digital and social media, political advertising, and polarization.
